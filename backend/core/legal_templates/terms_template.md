## Who runs this

This service is run by {operator}. You can reach us at {contact}. OpenOptOut is free, open-source software. The people who wrote it don't run this service and can't see your details.

## What it does

We use OpenOptOut to ask data brokers (websites and companies that collect and sell personal details) to remove your details. It sends opt-out requests on your behalf and checks back later to see whether your listings are gone.

## What it can't do

- It can't remove everything. It knows about the brokers on its list. New ones appear every day, and many aren't covered. It doesn't touch government or court records, news, or social media.
- Brokers decide. Some ignore requests, take months, or ask for more than we will send. Some put listings back later, which is why it checks again.
- Mistakes can happen. To opt you out, we have to send your details to the broker. We have safeguards, but a broker's site can change owners or change its form between checks.
- It is a best effort. We don't promise that your details will be removed, or stay removed.

- Only add other people if you're allowed to act for them: they've agreed, or you're their parent or legal guardian.
- Tell us if something looks wrong, for example a request sent to the wrong site.

## Your details

- We send them only to the data brokers OpenOptOut asks to remove you, and only what each one needs.
- We never send your Social Security number, ID numbers or passwords.
