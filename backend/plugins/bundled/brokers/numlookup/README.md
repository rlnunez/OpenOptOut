## What happens

NumLookup can take up to 45 days. Its manual request form has asked for a photo ID; OpenOptOut never uploads ID for you.

## The checklist steps

> Open https://www.numlookup.com/opt_out, enter one of these numbers, finish the robot check and choose to remove your information: {all_phones}. Choose to send a removal request by hand (you don't need an account) and fill in {full_name}, {contact_email} and the number. The form may ask for a photo ID. You can black out everything on it except your name and photo. Then press "I've done this".
