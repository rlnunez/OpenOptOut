## What happens

Usually a few business days. An employer that uploads its contact lists to ZoomInfo can bring you back, so it's rechecked.

## The checklist steps

> Open https://privacy.zoominfo.com/, look up {full_name} with your employer, choose the listing and ask for it to be removed. Verify with each work email ZoomInfo asks about. Your saved addresses: {all_emails}. Then press "I've done this".
