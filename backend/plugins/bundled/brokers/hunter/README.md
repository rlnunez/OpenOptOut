## The checklist steps

> Hunter shows people's work email addresses. Open https://hunter.io/ and click "Do Not Sell My Info" at the bottom. For each of these addresses, claim it, open the link Hunter sends to it, then press "Delete my information": {all_emails}. Then press "I've done this".
