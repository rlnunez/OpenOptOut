## What happens

Spy Dialer takes several names, addresses, numbers and emails in one go, so one visit covers every number.

## The checklist steps

> Open https://www.spydialer.com/ and click "Do Not Sell Or Share My Personal Information" at the bottom. Press Start, choose your state, finish the robot check, then add {full_name}, your address, each of these numbers ({all_phones}) and {email}. Press "Opt-Out My Info" and wait for "Your submitted data has been blocked from future sharing". Then press "I've done this".
