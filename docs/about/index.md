# About OpenOptOut

OpenOptOut is a free, self-hosted, AGPL-3.0 licensed tool dedicated to defending families and communities against intrusive commercial surveillance and unauthorized data brokerage.

## Sections

- **[Our Mission](mission.md)**  
  Why OpenOptOut exists: privacy as a universal human right, open-source principles, zero monetization of user data, and total user sovereignty.
- **[Privacy Legislation](legislation.md)**  
  A detailed review of global and state privacy laws (CCPA/CPRA, GDPR, LGPD, PIPEDA, state opt-out registries) and the statutory mandates that empower your right to be forgotten.
